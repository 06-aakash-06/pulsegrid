# Browser interaction and visual coverage

The browser used the real, non-mock frontend at `http://127.0.0.1:19173` against the isolated gateway at `:18880`. Vite mock, demo mock and local-hospital-demo flags were false. The previous audit had already visited all 35 routes in eight roles; Phase 10 used those route checks as its baseline instead of repeating the sweep.

All eight role dashboards were captured at 1536×1024, 1440×900, 1280×800 and 390×844 (32 screenshots). The measured document width equaled the viewport in all 32 samples. Emergency and Operations pages were recaptured after settling from their initial loading state. The Receptionist booking/walk-in path was captured at all four sizes; its document width matched every viewport. Pharmacy Inventory and Prescription Fulfillment were each captured at desktop and mobile sizes, with no horizontal document overflow. The Pharmacy desktop captures show the real hospital architecture background and the existing role layout. Evidence and per-capture measurements are in `evidence/role-responsive.json`, `evidence/walkin-responsive.json`, `evidence/secondary-responsive.json` and `evidence/browser/`.

Live browser interactions used isolated fictional records: Patient profile edit/review/confirm and stale ETag rejection; grant review/create/revoke with Doctor history access returning 200 before revocation and 404 afterwards; Receptionist patient lookup, demographics edit, booking review/confirmation and audited manual check-in; and Pharmacist availability filter, live medicine autocomplete with ArrowDown/Enter selection, required-expiry validation, stock review/confirmation and refreshed synthetic inventory quantity. Pharmacy screens and actions are recorded in `evidence/browser-actions.json`; the stock result is visible in [the saved screenshot](evidence/browser/pharmacy-stock-adjustment-success.jpg). The prior completed booking and grant screenshots are also retained in that folder.

The role-control inventory records reachable controls on sampled pages, not proof that each control was clicked. Browser console scans on the Receptionist, Operations and current Pharmacist sessions reported no errors or warnings. The Pharmacy required-field message appeared when the test form lacked an expiry, and confirmation remained blocked until a valid date was entered. No frontend source or teammate visual design was changed.

This is focused interaction and responsive evidence, not completion of the exhaustive audit requested for every route, control and state. Role-specific secondary routes for Doctor, Receptionist, Billing, Emergency, Administration and Operations were not all driven through every filter, form, modal, pagination state, keyboard path and refresh/back sequence. Focus states, reduced-motion behavior, GSAP/Motion cleanup, full-page modal overflow, error/empty/loading states and browser network failures were not exhaustively checked. The browser acceptance gate remains **PARTIAL**. The physical QR camera gate remains independently **BLOCKED**; see [device verification](03-qr-device-verification.md).
